Who We Are

One community. Many pathways. A world of opportunity.

OM Group of Companies is a highly diversified global business which owns a number of top performing entities mainly dealing in areas of finance, metals extraction & processing, oil & gas, international trading of commodities as well as human resource and education services.

These multidimensional businesses are spread across the globe with annual turnover of three billion US$. The group has significant presence and registered offices in London, Malaysia, Dubai, and Pakistan.

OM International is the flagship company of the group under which a number of successful and diverse business are operating.
